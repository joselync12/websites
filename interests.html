My Technology Interests

Exploring the digital frontier

Programming

Programming is the foundation of everything I love about technology. I enjoy the process of turning ideas into working software — writing clean code, debugging tricky problems, and seeing a project come to life. I'm currently building skills in Python, Java, and JavaScript.

UX/UI

UX/UI design fascinates me because it sits at the intersection of psychology, art, and technology. I love thinking about how people interact with digital products and how small design decisions can make a big difference in usability and enjoyment.

Cybersecurity

In a world where our lives are increasingly digital, cybersecurity is more important than ever. I'm interested in how we can protect people from threats like phishing, malware, and data breaches — and how awareness is the first line of defense.

Game Design

Game design is where creativity meets technology. I'm drawn to the challenge of building immersive worlds, designing engaging mechanics, and telling interactive stories. Games have the power to entertain, educate, and connect people.

Web Development

Web development is a skill I'm actively building. This very website is a project to practice my front-end skills! I love the idea that anyone with a browser can access what you build — the web is the most democratic platform there is.

Artificial Intelligence

AI is transforming every industry, from healthcare to entertainment. I'm curious about how AI can be used responsibly and securely — and how it will shape the future of work, creativity, and problem-solving.

Reflection Questions

What technology do I use most often?

I use web browsers and development tools most often, as they are essential for coding, researching, and building projects. My smartphone is also a daily tool for communication, learning, and creativity.

What technology skill would I like to improve?

I'd like to improve my front-end development skills, particularly in creating responsive, accessible, and visually appealing user interfaces. I also want to deepen my understanding of cybersecurity best practices.

What technology career interests me?

I'm interested in careers that blend technology and creativity, such as UX/UI design, front-end development, or cybersecurity analysis. I want a career where I can solve meaningful problems and make technology more accessible to everyone.

Why am I interested in technology?

I'm interested in technology because it empowers people. It gives us tools to create, communicate, solve problems, and make the world better. I want to be part of that — building things that matter and helping others navigate the digital world safely.
